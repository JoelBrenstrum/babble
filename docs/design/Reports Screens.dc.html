Reports
Families build their own charts from menus and chips: pick a measure, a range, a grouping and a split, and the preview updates live. Saved reports are shared with every carer. A free family saves 2. The chart types are on the component sheet (R01–R11).
How the builder folds onto a phoneThe desktop panel's seven sections become five tabs in a half-height sheet: Measure, Range (with Group by), Split (with Filters), Chart, and More (Options). One tab shows at a time, so the preview stays visible above the sheet and redraws as chips change. Save and Cancel stay fixed in the sheet footer. On tablet the panel stays beside the preview, and the sidebar collapses to the rail.
Which charts need widthThe heatmap and the rhythm chart pack 24 hours across, so they take the full card width at every size, and on the dashboard they default to M. On a phone the heatmap cells shrink to about 12px wide. The rhythm chart shows 7 rows and scrolls for 14. Bars past about 30 buckets merge into weeks. Donut, number and histogram read well at S.
