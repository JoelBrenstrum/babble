babble
Design system v1 · Sage
Tokens, components and core screens for the babble baby tracker. Two apps share these tokens: the mobile app (Expo, React Native and NativeWind, for iOS and Android) and the web app (TanStack Start with Tailwind and shadcn/ui on Radix). The web app also installs as a phone PWA, using the native layouts at phone width. Derived from the Organic system.

Palette
The primary colour is Organic's sage, a low-arousal olive that reads as a health app rather than a toy. Terracotta stays as the secondary for warmth and emphasis. Neutrals carry a faint warm-green tint. Dark mode sits on #141612 with #e3e2d3 text instead of white, which keeps 14:1 contrast without glare. No surface uses blue.
Tracker colours
The eight hues are spread around the wheel. In light mode each solid colour is at least 4.2:1 against the background, and each on-colour is at least 6.5:1 on its tint. In dark mode the solids become muted pastels. Left (clay) and Right (amber) are related but differ in hue, and always carry their L or R letter.
States and caution
Downtime is a dashed neutral marked "idle", so it reads as time passing rather than a fault. Medical caution uses a sand tone with a stethoscope icon and plain wording, and is kept separate from warning and danger. Poo swatches are fixed real-world colours that never change with the theme, and each has an edge ring so black and white stay visible.
Type and shape
Figtree is used throughout. It is humanist, with open shapes that stay readable at small sizes. Timers use tabular figures (fontVariant: tabular-nums) at weight 500, with smaller units in secondary ink so the digits lead. Caprasimo appears only in the wordmark. Corner radii are 16 for cards, 14 for buttons and full pills for chips. Touch targets are at least 48pt, and primary actions are 56pt.
